# Privacy

D2RR Item Database runs entirely on the user's computer.

- It does not make network requests.
- It does not collect or transmit analytics, telemetry, account data, character
  data, save files, or search history.
- It reads only its bundled `database.json` and `guides.json` files at runtime.
- It does not write to game saves or modify gameplay data.

GitHub and the Reimagined Hub may collect their own normal website or download
logs under their respective privacy policies. Those services are separate from
the installed plugin.
